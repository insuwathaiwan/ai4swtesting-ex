# Activity 4 – Step 2: Add an edge case

**Chatbot / model:** Claude (Opus 5.5)

## Prompt

```text
You will be given a list of foods delimited by three hashes. Extract all the fruits and write them out as a list.
###
bread, cheese, carrot, rice, chicken, tomato, strawberry yogurt, fruit cake
###
```

## Response

Tomato
Strawberry

Notes on the edge cases:

Tomato is botanically a fruit, though it’s usually treated as a vegetable in cooking.
Strawberry comes from “strawberry yogurt.” The yogurt itself isn’t a fruit, but it contains one.
Fruit cake is left out because it’s a baked good. Its specific fruits aren’t named.
